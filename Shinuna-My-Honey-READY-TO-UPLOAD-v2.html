For my Honey

A letter from your Darlie

My Honey,

If I could put every feeling I have for you into one place, it would still not be enough. I love your voice, your smile, the way you talk, your beautiful personality and even those endless conversations ambazo wakati mwingine zinaweza kuendelea mpaka usiku sana.

I love the girl who dreams of becoming a great Safety Officer. I love the girl who can be a little shy, then suddenly become the funniest and most talkative person in the room.

We have had distance, challenges and moments that tested us. But here we are. Still writing our story. InshaAllah, one day we'll look back and say: “We did it together.”

Forever yours,
AKHSAL — your Darlie ♥

Chapter by chapter

Our Story

From one unexpected reply to a love I never want to forget.

01
The Beginning

One message changed everything

I sent a greeting to your mother. Somehow, you were the one who replied. We kept talking, and little by little, our conversations became something more beautiful. Then came the words: “Shinuna nakupenda.”

02
The First Light

The night the electricity went off

That night, I was sent to the meter to borrow emergency electricity. You came to help and used your phone flashlight to illuminate me. A simple moment, but one I will always remember. Sometimes the smallest light becomes part of the biggest story.

03
A Walk Together

Mwanakwerekwe memories

I once waited for you after college and walked with you toward Mwanakwerekwe market. We talked and talked. With you, even a normal walk feels like a memory worth keeping.

04
Another Chapter

Darajani Souk

At Darajani, you escorted me while I looked for fabrics. We eventually sat together and watched videos on your phone. Simple. Quiet. Ours.

Our Next Chapter

Still choosing us

The story is not finished. InshaAllah, after four years, AKHSAL and Shinuna will look back at everything and smile: “We did it together.”
